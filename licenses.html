Pokyxel — Licenses & Privacy

Privacy

Privacy. Pokyxel runs entirely in your browser (or, as a desktop app, entirely on your computer). It collects no personal data, sends nothing to any server, sets no cookies, and makes no third-party network requests: the application, its fonts and its icons are served from this site itself and nothing else is loaded. Your drawings — the documents you save in Pokyxel, your autosave drafts, your recent-files list and your settings — are stored only in your browser's own local storage on your device (IndexedDB database pokyxel, localStorage keys beginning with pokyxel.) and never leave it. When you first save a document, Pokyxel asks the browser to keep that storage persistent so it is not cleared to free space; a browser may still refuse or clear it, so use File → Download a copy for anything you must keep. After your first visit the browser also keeps a copy of the application's own files (never your drawings) in its cache storage, under names beginning with pokyxel-, so Pokyxel opens without a connection and can be installed as an app. Help → Forget everything deletes all of it, your saved documents and the offline copy included. There is no tracking and no analytics.

This site is hosted on GitHub Pages at https://pokyno.github.io/pokyxel/. Like any web host, GitHub may record your IP address in standard server logs when the site is loaded; see GitHub's privacy statement (https://docs.github.com/site-policy/privacy-policies/github-general-privacy-statement). Pokyxel itself never sees those logs.

Accessibility

Pokyxel is usable with a single pointer and no keyboard, uses touch targets of at least 40 px and text contrast of at least 4.5 : 1, and never conveys information by color alone. As a non-commercial project it is outside the scope of the European Accessibility Act; it follows the Act's intent where it can.
